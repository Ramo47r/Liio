How do we protect your child's data?
The nickname is stored locally on this device and transmitted with each prompt so Lio can address your child personally. Please use a nickname only, not a full real name.

Messages are processed in real time by our AI & voice providers and are never stored permanently or used to train AI models. We do not store chat histories on our servers (COPPA & GDPR Privacy-by-Design).
